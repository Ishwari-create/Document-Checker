Time study: by hand vs with the desk

This measures the time saved instead of guessing it. Each person checks receipts in two ways: by hand (the receipt and the application only, as today) and assisted (the same, plus the system's findings). Every file is timed from opening to submitting, and what the person marked is compared with the defect that was really planted.

For a fair result: use 3 or more people, at least 10 receipts each in each mode, and let half of them start with the assisted mode. Receipts are drawn from the synthetic test set, so nobody knows the answer in advance.

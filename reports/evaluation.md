# Evaluation on the synthetic test set

24 documents (10 clean, 14 with one planted defect, 4 scanned). Generated by `tools/generate_test_docs.py`; truth in `data/labels.csv`.

These are synthetic receipts from three invented bank layouts. The numbers show that the method works end to end; they do not show performance on real AICTE files.

## Headline

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Document-level accuracy (PASS/FAIL/REVIEW matches truth) | 96% |
| Planted defects caught by the right rule | 14/14 |
| Defective documents passed as clean | 0 |
| Clean documents wrongly failed (false alarms) | 0 of 10 |
| Clean documents sent to an officer for review | 1 of 10 |
| Time per document: digital PDF / scan | 0.02 s / 0.99 s |
